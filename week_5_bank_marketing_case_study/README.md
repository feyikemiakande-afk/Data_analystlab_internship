# 📊 Term Deposit Subscription Analysis | Bank Marketing Case Study

A comprehensive business analytics case study exploring the factors that influence customer subscriptions to term deposits using a real-world bank marketing dataset.

This project applies exploratory data analysis (EDA), business intelligence techniques, and data visualisation to identify high-converting customer segments and provide strategic recommendations that can improve future marketing campaign performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Banks invest significant resources in direct marketing campaigns to promote financial products such as term deposits. However, not every customer responds positively, making effective customer targeting essential.

This analysis investigates customer demographics, financial characteristics, and previous campaign performance to answer two key business questions:

- What factors drive customers to subscribe to a term deposit?
- Which customer segments should the bank prioritise in future marketing campaigns?

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify the strongest predictors of term deposit subscriptions.
- Analyse customer behaviour across demographic, financial, and campaign variables.
- Compare subscription rates with customer account balances.
- Deliver actionable business recommendations to improve future marketing campaigns.

## 📊 Key Findings

### Previous campaign success is the strongest predictor

Customers who previously subscribed recorded a **91.3% subscription rate**, making historical campaign performance the most reliable predictor of future conversions.

### Older customers subscribe more frequently

Customers aged **60 years and above** achieved the highest subscription rate (**82.2%**), considerably outperforming younger age groups.

### Campaign timing influences performance

Marketing campaigns conducted during **March, September, October, and December** consistently achieved higher subscription rates than campaigns conducted during May, despite May having the highest number of customer contacts.

### Education level affects subscription behaviour

Customers with tertiary education were more likely to subscribe than those with primary or secondary education.

### High account balances do not guarantee higher conversions

The customer groups holding the largest total account balances were rarely the groups with the highest subscription rates. This demonstrates that **subscription rate is a more effective metric for customer targeting than financial value alone.**

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Prioritise customers with successful previous campaign outcomes.
- Increase marketing efforts toward customers aged 60+, students, and retirees.
- Optimise campaigns during high-performing months.
- Prioritise cellular communication channels.
- Use subscription rate rather than total account balance as the primary customer targeting metric.
