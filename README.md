## Week 8
### Week 8: Final Capstone – Global Urbanization & Population Density (WDI)
**Tools Used:**
- Excel
- Python (Pandas, Google Colab)
- Power BI

An end-to-end capstone analyzing global urbanization and population density trends using the World Bank's World Development Indicators (WDI) dataset, under the theme Environment: Density & Urbanization. Two interactive Power BI dashboards were built — Urbanization Trends and Density and Livability — alongside a written report with insights and recommendations.

**Key Insights:**
- 59.43% of the global population lives in urban areas, versus 40.57% rural.
- 154 of 217 countries (71%) have crossed the 50% urban-majority threshold as of 2024.
- Urban population growth has slowed, from 2.13% annually (2000–2012) to 1.73% annually (2013–2024).
- Urbanization is highly uneven by region, ranging from 87.0% in North America to 34.3% in South Asia.
- Slum population % falls sharply as income rises, from 65.94% in Low income countries to 3.97% in High income countries.
